Play on your Kobo

Tap to solve all of these in KOReader. One-time setup, on a computer:

  1. Download the Briefing puzzles plugin and unzip it.
  2. Plug in your Kobo and copy the briefinggames.koplugin folder into .adds/koreader/plugins. (.adds is a hidden folder; on a Mac press Cmd+Shift+. to see it.)
  3. Eject, then restart KOReader.
  4. Open the top menu → Tools (🛠) → Briefing puzzles. It downloads the day's puzzles over Wi-Fi.
